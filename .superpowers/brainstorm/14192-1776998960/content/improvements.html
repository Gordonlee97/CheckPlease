CheckPlease — Improvement Ideas

Select any that appeal to you. We'll design the ones you pick.

🧭 Clarity & Navigation

1

Step progress bar

A row of labelled dots at the top — People · Scan · Review · Assign · Summary — so users always know where they are and how many steps remain. Tapping a completed step could also jump back to it.

2

Scan confidence hints

After scanning, flag items the AI was uncertain about with a subtle ⚠ icon in the Review step — so the user knows exactly what to double-check instead of eyeballing everything.

⚡ Efficiency

3

"Split equally" one-tap

A button in the Assign step that assigns every item to everyone with one tap — for groups that just want to go Dutch without thinking about it. Individual items can still be reassigned after.

4

"Everyone" per-item shortcut

Each item card in Assign gets a small "All" pill alongside the person buttons. One tap assigns that item to the whole group — great for shared dishes like bread, apps, or a bottle of wine.

5

Quick tip selector

When tip is $0 in the Review step, show a row of preset buttons — 15% · 18% · 20% · Custom — that auto-fill the tip field and update the total instantly. No mental math required.

6

Remembered names

The "Who's splitting?" step remembers names from past splits and surfaces them as quick-add chips. Start typing "Gor" and "Gordon" appears. Frequent groups form in seconds.

🎨 Aesthetics & Colour

7

Person colour coding

Each person auto-gets a warm accent colour (amber, rose, teal, etc.) that works with the dark theme. Their Assign pills, Summary cards, and breakdown rows carry their colour — making the whole split instantly scannable at a glance.

✨ Juice & Delight

8

Step transitions

Smooth slide-in / slide-out animations when moving between steps — forward slides right, back slides left. Makes the flow feel like a real native app instead of pages popping in and out.

9

Summary celebration moment

A brief, tasteful animation when the Summary screen appears — gold confetti, a shimmer across the cards, or the totals counting up. Marks the satisfying end of the flow. Apps like Stripe and Duolingo nail this.

10

Venmo / payment deep links

On each person's Summary card, a "Pay via Venmo" button that opens the Venmo app pre-filled with their exact amount and a note like "Dinner at Nobu — CheckPlease". Closes the loop between splitting and actually getting paid.
