How does the shareable summary work?

After everyone's items are assigned, how do you get the breakdown to your group chat?

A

Encoded link → group chat

The full session data gets compressed into a URL. One tap opens the native share sheet (iMessage, WhatsApp, etc.) and pastes the link. Friends tap it and see a read-only breakdown page — their items, the math, the total they owe.

Pros

  • No backend storage needed — data lives in the URL
  • Link is permanent and self-contained
  • Works in any group chat
  • Friends can bookmark/save their own copy

Cons

  • Long URL (but hidden behind share sheet)
  • Needs a domain to host the breakdown page
B

Formatted text summary → copy/paste

Generates a clean text breakdown — like a mini receipt — that you copy and paste into any chat. No link, no app needed to read it. Just text.

🧾 CheckPlease — Dinner Apr 22
Gordon: Burger $14 + Fries $4
→ subtotal $18 + tax $1.62 + tip $3.24
→ YOU OWE: $22.86

Pros

  • Dead simple, no tech required
  • Works in any chat, email, SMS

Cons

  • No interactive breakdown
  • Harder to verify the math
C

Both — link + text fallback

Share the encoded link via the native share sheet (tap once, goes to group chat). Also offer a "copy text" button for people who just want a plain summary. Best of both worlds.

Pros

  • Link gives full interactive breakdown
  • Text fallback works everywhere
  • No backend needed

Cons

  • Slightly more UI to build
