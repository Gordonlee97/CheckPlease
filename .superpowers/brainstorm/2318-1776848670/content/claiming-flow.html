How do people claim their items?

After the receipt is scanned, someone has to assign items to each person. Who does that work, and how?

A

One person assigns everything

The phone owner scans the receipt, then taps each item and picks who ordered it from a list of names. Everyone else just waits for the result.

Pros

  • Fastest — no coordination needed
  • No sharing / link flow required
  • One person stays in control

Cons

  • Phone owner has to remember who ordered what
  • Error-prone for large groups
B

Pass the phone around

After scanning, each person gets the phone and taps their own items. Simple in-person flow, no accounts or links needed.

Pros

  • Each person claims their own — no guessing
  • No accounts or shared links needed
  • Fun, tactile group activity

Cons

  • Requires physically passing the phone
  • Awkward for 6+ people
C

Shareable link — everyone claims on their own device

After scanning, a unique link is generated. Each person opens it on their own phone and taps what they ordered. Real-time sync shows what's been claimed.

Pros

  • Scales to any group size
  • Everyone participates simultaneously
  • No passing the phone

Cons

  • Requires a backend + real-time sync
  • Everyone needs to open a link
  • More complex to build
